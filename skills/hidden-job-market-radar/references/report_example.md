# Report example

This shows the expected format and level of detail of a RADAR report. Every company, person, figure and link here is fictional.

---

**Subject:** Job Radar - 2 fits

## 1. Summary

14 openings verified this week: **2 FIT** (≥70), **1 NEAR** (65–69). One confirmed executive search mandate and two new partners. Best opportunity: CRO at Northwind Logistics Software (90). Main signal: three logistics software companies raised growth rounds this quarter and are building commercial leadership.

## 2. Fits

| Company | Role | Location / mode | Compensation | Score | Main reason | Main gap | Experience | Source | Contact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Northwind Logistics Software | Chief Revenue Officer | Madrid, hybrid | EUR 160–190K + 40% variable (published) | 90 | Same model and scale; owns all revenue; mandate to double ARR | No board-level reporting yet | Direct | Company ATS | Ana R., former colleague, now Head of Product |
| Contoso Analytics | VP Sales EMEA | Remote, Spain | EUR 140–170K base (ESTIMATE, medium) | 80 | Build the European team from 8 to 40 | Reports to US CRO, not CEO | Direct (team scaling), transferable (new region) | Company careers page | None |

## 3. Near the bar

- **Tailspin Payments, Head of Sales Spain (67).** Strong fintech transferability, but a country role and a published range of EUR 115–130K keep it below 70. Worth a conversation only if the scope includes Portugal and LATAM.

## 4. Reopenings and adjustments

- **Fabrikam Industrial IoT, Commercial Director**: first seen 12 Sep; reappeared 3 Oct with the reporting line changed from the COO to the CEO and a new range. Likely the first search failed and the role was upgraded. Score raised from 68 to 72.

## 5. Mandates, signals and contacts

- **CONFIRMED MANDATE.** Example Search Partners (fictional) is running a CRO search for a European B2B SaaS company. Partner: Laura M., technology practice. Route: public professional email on the firm's site. Message drafted (not sent):
  > Hello Laura, I lead commercial teams in B2B SaaS and grew ARR from EUR 12M to EUR 31M in four years...
- **HIRING SIGNAL.** Litware Freight raised a EUR 40M Series B (press release, 28 Sep) to expand in Southern Europe. No commercial leadership role published yet. Your contact Pablo G. works there in Finance: warm-introduction request drafted (not sent).

## 6. Applications

- **Contoso Analytics, VP Sales EMEA**: applied 18 Sep, no update in 17 days. Follow-up drafted (not sent).
- **Fabrikam Industrial IoT**: stage changed to *interview* on 2 Oct. Interview brief ready on request.

## 7. What the market is hiring for

- Four of the five FIT and NEAR openings ask for experience building a partner or channel sales model. Your CV mentions a partner programme only in one line. If it's accurate, consider adding the result it delivered to your CV and LinkedIn summary.
- Two isolated mentions of product-led growth: not a trend yet.

## 8. Excluded

- Level below target (individual contributor or team lead): 6
- Published maximum below EUR 110K: 3
- Posting older than 45 days and not confirmed open: 2

## 9. Coverage

- Sources with results: 3 company ATS feeds, 2 executive search portals, web discovery.
- Sources without results: generalist aggregator (1 query, 0 useful; third run in a row, demoted).
- Failures: one careers page timed out twice, marked for revalidation.
- New: Ashby feed for Contoso Analytics added to `company_universe.md`; P2 cohort B reviewed (8 companies).
- Next run: review P2 cohort C; check Litware Freight's careers page for new roles.
