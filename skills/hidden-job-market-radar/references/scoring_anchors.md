# Scoring anchors

Calibrate against these worked examples before scoring the first opening of a run, so scores mean the same thing from one week to the next. Every company and person here is fictional. The candidate profile is an example; apply the same reasoning to the real candidate.

**Example candidate.** Commercial director at a mid-size B2B software company (EUR 40M revenue). Ten years in B2B SaaS sales leadership, leads a team of 45, owns new-business revenue. Grew annual recurring revenue from EUR 12M to EUR 31M in four years. Based in Madrid, fluent in Spanish and English. Target: VP Sales / Chief Revenue Officer. Target base EUR 150K; exclude below EUR 110K.

| Score | Verdict | Example opening | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 90 | FIT | **CRO, Northwind Logistics Software** (Series C, EUR 60M revenue, Madrid or remote Spain). Owns all revenue, reports to the CEO, mandate to scale from EUR 60M to EUR 120M. Published range EUR 160–190K + 40% variable. | Same business model and scale; owns the full outcome; direct experience in every core requirement; compensation above target; clear step up. |
| 80 | FIT | **VP Sales EMEA, Contoso Analytics** (US scale-up opening Europe). Builds the European team from 8 to 40. No published salary; estimate EUR 140–170K base (ESTIMATE, medium confidence). | Direct experience in building and scaling teams; new region adds risk; reports to a US CRO rather than the CEO, so less ownership. |
| 72 | FIT | **Commercial Director, Fabrikam Industrial IoT** (hardware plus software, EUR 200M revenue). Owns new business for the software line. | Business problem matches (professionalise a software sales channel), but hardware sales cycles and buyer are transferable, not direct experience. Scope is a business line, not the whole company. |
| 67 | NEAR | **Head of Sales Spain, Tailspin Payments** (fintech, EUR 25M revenue in Spain). | Strong sector transferability, but it is a country role inside a larger organisation: scope and title are a step sideways. Published range EUR 115–130K, below target. |
| 55 | Out | **Sales Director, Wide World Retail** (consumer retail chain). | B2C store sales: different customer model and buyer. The argument would rely on generic leadership only. |
| 30 | Out | **Senior Account Executive, Adventure Works SaaS** | Individual contributor role: level clearly below target. A knockout filter would normally exclude it before scoring. |

## How to read a score

- **85–100**: direct experience in the core of the role, ownership of the outcome, compensation at or above target, coherent progression. The candidate would be a front-runner.
- **70–84**: credible and differentiated, with one clear gap (transferable rather than direct experience in one area, less ownership, or compensation uncertain).
- **65–69**: interesting but held back by a gap that matters (scope, level, sector distance, compensation). Explain what keeps it below 70.
- **Below 65**: not a credible application without stretching the story. Log it; don't rank it.

Keyword overlap alone never makes a FIT: score the business problem and the ownership of the outcome first.
